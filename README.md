# Xenon Emulator
Xenon Emulator is a retro game emulator built to easily play old video games on the web. Upload your ROM files, and start playing immediately.

---
## Support for 16+ Consoles
With emulation powered by RetroArch, you can easily emulate games from 16+ different consoles, including the NES, SNES, N64, PlayStation 1, SEGA Genesis, etc (including 3DS and PSP!).

---
## Controller Support
All games have controller support just like the original. Just connect a controller via BlueTooth or USB‍-‍A / USB‍-‍C and you should be able to connect a controller to the game.

---
## Cloud Saves
All your progress and preferences are saved to the cloud automatically so don't worry about losing progress on new devices. You can also choose to inject save states or locally backup save states.
