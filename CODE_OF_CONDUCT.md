# Code of Conduct
---

Our commitment is to ensure that this is a safe, welcoming and inclusive space for contributors, where no one is harassed or targeted individually. by anyone.

---
## Expected Behaviour
This applies to _every_ contributor.
* Being respectful in discussions.
* Giving feedback regarding code &mdash; not individual people.
* Helping others

## Unacceptable Behaviour
The following is unacceptable behaviour for all contributors.
* Harassment
* Discrimination
* Personal attacks
  * (and similar)
* Trolling and insulting comments
* Publishing private information or any information without permission.

---
Instances of unacceptable behaviour may result to notifying project maintainers, removal of such comments, and restriction to participation toward contribution.
If you have any concerns about breach of this **Code of Conduct**, please reach out to project maintainers (contacts listed in README.md).

---
## Scope
These rules apply to all contributors to this project, including this repository, comments, issues, pull requests, and more.
